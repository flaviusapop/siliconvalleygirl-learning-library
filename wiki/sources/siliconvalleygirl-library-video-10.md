## Actionable learnings

1. Pick a small recurring pain point, try a manual AI-assisted pass and inspect the result before adding a connector.
2. After repeating a workflow several times, document the steps and consider automating only the parts you understand.
3. For a product idea, list the stakeholders and their different needs, safeguards and trust requirements before building.
4. Measure a workflow against its actual customer outcome rather than treating token volume or code output as proof of productivity.
