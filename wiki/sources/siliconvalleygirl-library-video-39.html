Actionable learnings

  1. Choose a recurring decision and document the relevant context, then use a model to challenge assumptions rather than accepting its answer as fact.
  2. Build a small reusable voice guide using real examples and verified facts, and review drafts before publishing.
  3. Try one scheduled, low-risk briefing or follow-up draft, checking its sources and recipients before taking action.
  4. Prototype a narrow customer problem, research alternatives, test the result and bring in technical expertise before scaling.
