## Actionable learnings

1. Ask an assistant to interview you about a recent workweek and suggest specific use cases; share only information appropriate to the tool and your permissions.
2. Document one recurring process as reusable instructions, test it on a real task, then review any proposed instruction changes against the outcome.
3. Before connecting an agent to workplace data, map access permissions and require a human to review consequential messages and decisions.
4. Compare the cost and usefulness of a candidate automation with the manual workflow; stop or redesign it if the output is not used.
