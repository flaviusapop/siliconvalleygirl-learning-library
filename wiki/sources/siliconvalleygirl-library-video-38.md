## Actionable learnings

1. Select a familiar industry and document a real process improvement, including the method and actual before-and-after measurements, before offering consulting.
2. Ask multiple AI search tools about selected local businesses and investigate missing or inaccurate results as a starting point for discovery, not a promise of placement.
3. Interview potential customers in one vertical about missed calls or appointment friction before piloting a voice receptionist and negotiating an appropriate service.
4. Prototype a narrowly defined workflow and test it with potential users; evaluate willingness to pay alongside delivery costs, reliability and customer needs.
